Gyeongjin Kang

I am currently a research assistant at Yonsei University. Previously, I was an MS student (2024.03 ~ 2026.02) at Sungkyunkwan University. I have broad interests in Computer Vision. My current focus is on (1) self-supervised geometric perception, (2) multimodal representation learning for spatial understanding, and (3) scalable perception from continuous streams for embodied and real-world applications.
